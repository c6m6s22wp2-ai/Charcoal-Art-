The Artist

Discipline
Meets Art

As a senior in high school, I have spent most of my life balancing discipline, competition, and creativity. I am a three-time Lower State wrestling champion and a 5A state finalist — experiences that have taught me perseverance, focus, and dedication both on and off the mat.

Living on Daniel Island, I began exploring art as a way to express emotion, tell stories, and challenge myself creatively. Working primarily with charcoal and drawing materials, I enjoy creating detailed pieces that capture depth, realism, and personal meaning.

My background in wrestling influences my artistic process through the patience, discipline, and work ethic I bring into every piece I create.
